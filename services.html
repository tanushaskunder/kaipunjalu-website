Our Services

Services designed for our members and community

What We Provide

💰

Deposits

We provide information and services related to deposits for our members and customers.

🏦

Loans

Loan-related services are provided to meet the financial needs of members and customers.

📱

SMS Alerts

SMS alerts provide quick and timely communication with members and customers.

🛡️

Life Insurance

LIC Micro Insurance Policies are made available to members through the society's branches.

❤️

Health Insurance

Health insurance services are offered in collaboration with Star Health and Allied Insurance.

🚗

Vehicle Insurance

Insurance benefits are provided to help protect the assets and vehicles of fishermen members.

❤️ Member Welfare

The society also provides support through the Death Relief Fund Scheme and medical assistance for economically weaker members.

Members are also provided with Manipal Arogya Cards to access medical services and discounts at partner hospitals.
