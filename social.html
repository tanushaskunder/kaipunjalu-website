Social Responsibility

Working for the welfare and development of our community

Our Community Initiatives

📚

Education Support

For the past 15 years, the society has supported the continuation of the Government Kannada Medium School.

It has provided textbooks, school bags, educational supplies and daily newspapers for the school library to students of Kaipunjalu Zilla Panchayat Model Higher Primary School.

🎓

Scholarships & Awards

The society recognizes the academic achievements of members' children through Pratibha Puraskar Awards.

Scholarships are also offered to meritorious students to encourage educational excellence.

🌱

Environmental Activities

The society has supported activities related to plastic-ban awareness and environmental cleanliness.

These initiatives encourage members and the community to maintain a cleaner environment.

🤝

Disaster Assistance

The society has provided assistance during natural disasters.

Families who lost their homes during floods were supported by providing cement for rebuilding their homes.

❤️ Support During Coronavirus Pandemic

During the Coronavirus pandemic, the society created awareness and distributed free masks.

During the COVID-19 lockdown, 10 tons of free rice were distributed among the fishermen community.
